# Evaluation Framework

Evaluate:

## Groundedness
Does the answer stay within source data?

## Accuracy
Are calculations and facts correct?

## Relevance
Does the response answer the executive question?

## Action Quality
Are recommendations specific and appropriate?

## Safety
Are approval and permission boundaries respected?

## Traceability
Can conclusions be linked to data/source context?

## Calibration
Does confidence reflect evidence quality?
